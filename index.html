Padel Progress

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

In short: everything you log in Padel Progress — matches, scores, player names, Americano sessions, goals and your name — stays on your phone. There is no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect or share any of your data.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Padel Progress Android app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The app is developed and published by George Tsakaleris, an independent developer. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information you enter in the app

The app lets you record:

  • matches (date, set scores, optional tournament/event name and notes);
  • names of partners and opponents that you type in;
  • Americano sessions (player names and round scores);
  • monthly goals;
  • optionally, your own name (used to greet you and to find your Americano results).

This information is stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage. We do not upload it, we cannot see it, and we do not share it with anyone. The app has no user accounts and no cloud sync.

3. How your data is stored and deleted

  • Your data remains on your phone until you delete it.
  • You can delete single matches or goals inside the app.
  • You can delete all app data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Padel Progress → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • If you lose or reset your phone, your data cannot be recovered by us, because we never had a copy.

4. No advertising, analytics or tracking

This version of the app shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking. It does not use your device’s advertising ID and does not build any profile about you. In this version the app does not send any of your data over the internet, and every feature works offline.

5. Permissions

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or location. When you use Share my stats, the app creates an image of your stats card and hands it to Android’s share menu; only the app you choose (for example WhatsApp) receives it, and only when you share it.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not collect, sell, rent or share your personal information. No third party receives data from the app.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Data security

Your padel data is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read under normal Android security. Because it never leaves your device, it is not exposed to server breaches. Please keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data.

  • For data stored in the app: you have full control — view, edit or delete it directly on your device.
  • You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Greece: the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, dpa.gr).

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy, for example if new features (such as ads, optional accounts or cloud sync) are added. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date before they take effect. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.

11. International users

Your padel data stays on your device wherever you are; it is not transferred to any country or server.
